# Fact-check round 1 — data/letters.json + data/units.json

Checked against `research/03_script_reference.md` (Wikipedia "Urdu alphabet" primary source),
`data/tatoeba_urd_freq.json` (recomputed letter frequencies), and general Pakistani-standard-Urdu
knowledge. Codepoints verified with `python3 -c "hex(ord(ch))"` against every `cp` field.
Data files were **not** edited (per instructions); some appeared to be under concurrent edit by
another process during this pass (unit 1's word list flipped between having "ماما"/"امام" between
reads) — findings below are against the snapshot read last, and should be re-verified against
whatever is on disk at fix time.

**Summary: every `cp` codepoint (38 letters + 6 diacritics + 4 punctuation + 10 numerals) is
correct. Every `joiner`/`never_initial` flag is correct (including the two fields that looked
wrong on a naive "10-non-joiner-list" check — ں and ء are separately marked NJ in the source
table, so `joiner:false` is right for both). All `loan` and `role` fields checked are correct.
The `tatoeba_pct` values are correct for every letter except one.**

## Verification method notes
- **Codepoints**: scripted check of every `cp` field in `letters.json` (letters, diacritics,
  punctuation, numerals) via `hex(ord(ch))` — 100% match, zero discrepancies.
- **tatoeba_pct**: recomputed `count / 72766 * 100` for every letter from
  `data/tatoeba_urd_freq.json`'s `letters` array and rounded to 1 decimal — only the hamza row
  (item 1 above) diverges.
- **joiner / never_initial**: scripted against the research doc's explicit per-letter J/NJ column
  and the "never begins a word" list (ں ھ ڑ ے) — all correct.
- **Example words begin with the letter**: scripted for all 38 letters; only the hamza row
  (item 2) fails, and it fails because it's a different codepoint, not a different letter.
- **Decodability**: scripted a cumulative-letters check across all 12 units' word lists against
  `letters.json`'s `unit` field, allowing for explicit "(preview)" gloss tags — surfaced the
  āمزہ-family gap (item 5).
- **Vowel-diacritic-vs-romanisation consistency**: scripted a scan of every 4th-field vowelled
  spelling in `units.json` for zer-before-ی and pesh-before-و sequences (the specific "no zer
  before ی for ī" pattern flagged in the brief), then manually parsed each hit syllable-by-syllable
  against its romanisation. Four of five hits (تکیا, پیالا, گڑیا, خوش) turned out to be **correct**
  — the zer/pesh belonged to the *preceding* consonant's own short vowel, not to ی/و as a long-vowel
  marker, which is legitimate. Only ہوا (item 4) was a genuine mismatch.
- **Rude/archaic/odd-for-beginner words**: manually reviewed all ~220 words across 12 units against
  frequency/register/concreteness; confirmed the four words the brief specifically flagged (ابے,
  بلا, انا, ٹال) are indeed weak choices and proposed unit-letter-constrained replacements for each.
