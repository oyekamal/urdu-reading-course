Urdu Reader · Privacy policy

App: Urdu Reader (package com.oyekamal.urdureader) · Developer: Muhammad Kamal, Pakistan · Effective 19 September 2026 (donate section added the same day)

The short version

Urdu Reader works completely offline. It has no account, no login, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything a learner does stays on the phone it was done on. We never see it.

What the app stores, and where

DataWhyWhere it lives
Learner name, chosen colour, track (child / adult / heritage), gradeTo show progress per learner on a shared phoneOn the device only (browser storage inside the app)
Lesson progress, quiz scores, review cards, reading speed, drill attemptsTo pick the next lesson and show the progress screenOn the device only
Teacher name and PIN, class roster, reading assessmentsTeacher mode: roster, lesson scripts, EGRA-style assessmentOn the device only. The PIN is stored on the device and is not a password to any online service.
Settings (script, text size, vowel marks, audio-first mode)Accessibility and display preferencesOn the device only

A name is the only piece of personal information the app asks for, and it can be anything, including a nickname or a single letter. It never leaves the device unless you export it yourself.

What the app does not do

  • No internet connection is needed or used for learning. The app requests the INTERNET permission only so that the optional links below can open in your browser.
  • No advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts, or file access. Reading-aloud drills are timed by tapping; nothing is recorded.
  • No accounts and no in-app purchases.

Sharing you start yourself

Two features send data out, and only when you tap them:

  • Export backup / Share report creates a file or text on your phone and hands it to the Android share sheet. Where it goes (WhatsApp, email, Drive) is your choice, under that app's privacy policy.
  • Help make this better (More tab) opens WhatsApp or GitHub in your browser to contact the developer. These links sit behind a simple grown-ups-only arithmetic question. Nothing is sent until you write a message there.
  • Donate (More tab, behind the same grown-ups question) shows the developer's Easypaisa mobile-wallet number and can copy it or open the Easypaisa app if you have it. Donations are voluntary, made entirely inside Easypaisa under its own terms, and unlock nothing in the app. The app never sees whether you donated.

Children

The app is made for children from about age 5 learning to read Urdu, together with the adults who teach them. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no personal information about a child is ever received by the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Reset this profile's progress in More, removes everything.

Audio

Letter, word and sentence sounds are pre-recorded files shipped inside the app. Some are produced with an open machine voice model under a non-commercial licence, as stated in the app. No speech is generated online.
