What changed in round 2

  1. Marko starts asleep; tap to wake him, and he greets you in Urdu.
  2. You pick his lucky colour, which becomes the learner's badge.
  3. Marko answers every choice with a new pose and a line of his own.
  4. Loader is a filling ring with four steps that tick off, named after your answers.
  5. بابا is now checked, not trusted: pick it out from با by ear.
  6. Day-1 streak (earned by reading) and a days-in-a-row promise before the plan.
