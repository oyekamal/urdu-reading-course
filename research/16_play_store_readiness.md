# Play Store Readiness Checklist (2026)

App context: offline Urdu phonics app; learner + teacher + parent modes; no accounts; no ads; all data local (IndexedDB); a share/export button; a planned WhatsApp contact link and a donate button in a "More" tab; children (5+) are the primary audience but adults (teachers/parents) also use it.

## Ordered launch sequence (first-time personal developer account)

1. **Create the Google Play Console personal developer account** (one-time $25 fee) and complete identity verification.
2. **Build the release**: bump target SDK to API 36, produce a signed `.aab`.
3. **Set up the app in Play Console**: enroll in Play App Signing on first upload.
4. **Fill mandatory declarations before any test track**: Data safety form, Content rating (IARC) questionnaire, Target audience & content section (select child + adult age groups), Privacy policy URL, App access, Ads declaration (none), News app declaration (no), Government app declaration (no), COVID-19 (no).
5. **Prepare store listing assets**: icon (512×512), feature graphic (1024×500), ≥2 phone screenshots, app name (≤30 chars), short description (≤80 chars), full description (≤4000 chars). Add tablet screenshots/promo video if ready (optional).
6. **Create a closed test track**, add ≥12 testers by email/Google Group, get them opted in, and let it run **14 uninterrupted days** with no data-safety/policy rejections.
7. **Apply for production access** in the Play Console dashboard once the 14 days complete (answer questions on test experience, app purpose/audience, readiness steps taken from feedback). Allow up to ~7 days for Google's review.
8. **On approval, promote the release to Production**, confirm country distribution includes Pakistan, confirm pricing is Free, and publish.
9. **Post-launch (optional):** apply for the Teacher Approved program once the Families-relevant policy compliance is stable.

Note: two items above (parental gate on the WhatsApp/donate link, and whether a donate button is permitted at all in a mixed-audience/children's app) were **not explicitly confirmed** in the official pages fetched during this research — the mitigations listed are precautionary, not policy-guaranteed. Re-verify against the live Families Policy Requirements page immediately before submission, since Play policy updates frequently.
