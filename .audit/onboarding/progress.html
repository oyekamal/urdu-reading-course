Done, missing, to-do (3 Oct)

Done built and checked

  • Privacy and branding: the voice vendor's name and your phone number are gone from everything that ships (source, web build, Android app, store bundle). Contact is an email button; donations are a bank-transfer card; both sit behind a typed spelled-out-number gate that a child still learning to read cannot pass.
  • Practice PDFs: 107 printable A4 files (tracing, look-alikes, join, read and match, reading, dictation, unit checks with answer keys, parent guides, alphabet chart, flashcards, certificate), opened from the Me tab, working offline. A fresh verifier found one layout overflow (unit 9) and it was fixed.
  • Heavier words: unit words 220 to 1,026; words of 4+ letters 49% to 70%; running words 270 to 1,582 across 189 sentences and 11 passages; 1,463 audio clips; 65% of the 1,000 most common real Urdu words now taught (was 14%).
  • Fixes from the validation test: screen-reader and keyboard fixes, contrast, large Urdu text, portrait lock; home-screen CPU 96% to about 6%, Lighthouse 0.68 to 0.93, a web version that updates itself and teacher mode that works offline; names can no longer run as code, backup import is strict with a Restore option, double-tap guards, database-failure recovery screen, locked units open as read-only previews; correct dot-count hints, aspirate audio, placement check, reading-test scoring, direction-aware tracing, a "days practised" counter that never resets.
  • Release 0.11.0: signed Play Store bundle (27.8 MB, signature verified), tester APK (29.9 MB), refreshed web version, README, store text and privacy policy updated, grown-up gate test all green, layout audit 0 problems (reported by the agents on their own copies).

Missing or not verified honest gaps

  • Final course test not finished: the full unit 0 to 12 run on the combined result is still in progress (at unit 4); I have not re-run the layout audit on the final combined tree myself.
  • No native-speaker review: an AI wrote and checked the new words, sentences and passages. 226 judgement calls are listed in data/REVIEW_NEEDED.md and 11 audio clips are marked unverified.
  • No real-phone test: TalkBack, the Android back gesture, soft-keyboard PIN entry, installing from the bundle, share and print. iOS untested. Only the emulator was used.
  • Store screenshots are from the old look and need refreshing before the Play listing goes live.
  • Small leftovers: the 11 passage clips have no play button yet; some text still says "Words 1 and 2"; speech bubbles and unit labels clip a little at 200% text; a rare double-tap edge case; the internet permission is kept because removing it could not be tested; Marko uses about 29% CPU for 4 seconds after a touch; largest content paint 3.2 s (target 3.0 s); unit 4 has 141 words (about 11 lessons), which may be long.
  • Public repo: developer scripts that name the voice vendor are tracked, and your old phone number is still in 7 old commits (cleaning history needs a force-push).
  • Mobbin: added but not signed in, so the comparison with similar apps used public sources only.
  • Features that similar apps have and we do not: illustrated decodable stories with comprehension questions, parent-set reminders, read-aloud practice with feedback, a human voice, a family dashboard, an Urdu/English interface switch, a tablet layout, a home-screen widget, a free-play letter toy.

What changed in round 2

  1. Marko starts asleep; tap to wake him, and he greets you in Urdu.
  2. You pick his lucky colour, which becomes the learner's badge.
  3. Marko answers every choice with a new pose and a line of his own.
  4. Loader is a filling ring with four steps that tick off, named after your answers.
  5. بابا is now checked, not trusted: pick it out from با by ear.
  6. Day-1 streak (earned by reading) and a days-in-a-row promise before the plan.
