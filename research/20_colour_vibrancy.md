# 20. Colour vibrancy pass (2026-10-21)

Request (Kamal): the app looks dull / low on colour; do not recolour Marko or replace the palette; research what looks best.

## Measurement (why it felt dull)
Same yardsticks on real screens: our app vs App Store screenshots of kids' apps (Hasler-Susstrunk colourfulness, % near-neutral pixels, % vivid pixels S>0.5,V>0.45).
| app | colourful | neutral % | vivid % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duolingo ABC | 52 | 31 | 59 |
| ABCmouse | 52 | 8 | 62 |
| Lingokids | 47 | 10 | 44 |
| Duolingo (main) | 34 | 73 | 17 |
| Khan Academy Kids | 33 | 18 | 40 |
| Endless Alphabet | 31 | 22 | 28 |
| Sago Mini | 35 | 10 | 21 |
| **Our app 0.13.8** | **11** | **90** | **3.4** |
Even Duolingo's mostly-white UI carries ~5x our saturated colour.

## Findings used (sources are secondary; confidence medium-low, see gaps)
- Neutrals carry utility, accents add delight (Duolingo palette wording, via blog hub). Tint cards by surface role at ~5-8% (Material 3 surface containers). One saturated colour per screen (SAP/M3 tonal docs).
- Define colour as semantic tokens (Khan Academy Wonder Blocks rebuild) -> `.hue-N` unit tokens and `body[data-tab]` tab tokens.
- Never colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1); text on saturated bands needs 4.5:1 (1.4.3): white only on the DEEP end of each hue, navy ink on saffron/cerulean/tangerine bands. Accent colour stays off body text.
- Children: moderate brightness comfortable, very low contrast hurts concentration (PMC10771309, toys not reading). Keep cream page + navy ink (Meares 1980; Rello and Bigham 2017 warm backgrounds). Honest gap: no study on saturation vs reading for 4-10.
- Heritage hues (names sourced, hex approximated): cobalt/lajvard, firozi, madder red, tomato, phulkari magenta, kashi purple, ralli pastels; avoid flag green as decoration, avoid saffron+white+green tricolour. Red/berry already mean WRONG in this app, so no red/magenta as unit hues.

## Decision: "Bazaar Day"
Unit hues (n % 7): teal, saffron, cobalt, plum, cerulean, leaf, tangerine. Tab bands use the colours the tab icons already have. Marko, base teal/saffron/indigo and feedback colours untouched. Generated by `scripts/gen_colour_css.py` (light + dark).
Result on the same screens: vivid 3.4% -> ~12%, neutral 90% -> ~80% (dark: 4% -> 11%).

## Gauntlet loop on the whole UI (same day)
Bar: blind pairs vs Khan Kids / Duolingo / Lingokids / Endless Alphabet / ABCmouse / Sago Mini store screenshots, sonnet critics. Round results (ours won/6): r1 0, r2 1 (Me vs Duolingo course list), r3 0. Commercial apps still win on illustration/mascot richness; those gaps are not CSS.
Objective gate added: `mobile/tools/ui_defects.py` (clipped/truncated text, sideways overflow, tap targets, contrast; 5 widths x light/dark). Found and fixed: unit header text hugging the edge (negative margins in a zero-padding card), lesson panel flush text, page scrolling sideways (parallax layers), desktop tab bar spread across 1280px, dark-mode speaker button label 1.7:1, tiny/wrapping path labels, Review single-button dock (half width), lesson content hidden under the CTA.
Lesson learned: haiku critics were too lenient on character fidelity (Marko); for "match our own art" and for taste, Kamal is the judge.
